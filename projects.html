Improving optical flow representations for 3D convolutions (INRIA)
Proposed a separable convolutional block instead of 3D convolutions for video classification by separating the spatial and temporal convolutions [(2+1)D]. Represented the optical flow as magnitude and orientation in HSV colour space to represent flow (temporal) information. Showed that it improved results for I3D network and improved upon the state of the art. Also conducted abalation studies with frame selection in videos for video representations.

Featuring the features for action recognition (INRIA)
Studying the contribution of using multiple video cues for representing appearance, short-term and pose based motion to reach a high action recognition rate. Proposed a two level fusion strategy to combine the features from different cues to address False positives and negatives of similar actions which are common in action recognition by conditional binary classifier to disambiguate them ensuring a sequential refinement of action labels. We use ResNet152 features for appearance, Improved dense trajectories for short term motion and skeleton with LSTM for long term motion. We validate our approach on four public datasets, CAD-60, CAD-120, MSRDailyActivity3D and NTU-RGB+D improving the state-of-the-art results on them.

Spatio-temporal Action grids (INRIA)
Addressing the challenge of spatio-temporal relationship in videos for action recognition. Introducing grids in the spatial domain as well as temporal domain for spatio-temporal modeling of features To represent temporal information, we divide the action sequence into temporal segments termed as temporal grids. Cross subject variance is targetted by applying clustering on the appearance features and velocity features to partition the temporal segments. This ensures consistency of the temporal segments for modeling the actions. Results were competitive with the state of the art for four datasets.

Document Image Binarization (ISI Kolkata)
Proposed a novel method for degraded document image binarization using a convolutional autoencoder with skip connections and ensembling of three global and local thresholding methods. Learned weighted voting for the decisions from three pipelines to obtain a binary decison for each pixel. Patch to patch binarization technique for document page. Achieved an average F-measure of 90.18 on DIBCO datasets within 1% of the state of the art but on a wider range of document problems. Involved in independent research at the handwriting recognition lab. To the best of my knowledge this was the first time deep learning was used for document binarization.

Human activity recognition using 3D convolutions (VIT)
Implemented a C3D like 3D convolutional neural network in keras and trained it on KTH and weizmann datasets. Compared it with hand crafted techniques like dense trajectories, HOG, STIP. Achieved State of the art results on the two datasets.

Facial Emotion recognition
Evaluated the performance of 20 different classification techniques for emotion recognition. Including CNN, SVM and KNN. Feature descriptors used were facial landmarks, Gabor filters, HOG, LBP and LDP. Achieved 93% accuracy with CNN on Jaffe dataset.

Adaptive Skin Segmentation using Histogram back projection The project aimed at adaptive skin colour segmentation by detecting faces. The face was recognized with a viola jones classifier and the region of interest was used in histogram back projection to create a mask of skin pixels in the frame.

Gene study of Gestational Diabetes Mellitus
Studied and found the most probable genes that cause GDM using the microarray data of the disease using Benjamini Hochberg method. Find the regulation of the genes and their interacting partners. 21 genes were recognized to influence diabetes.
