I am interested in computer vision, machine learning, optimization and ML algorithms. Particularly my work is associated with self-supervised learning, activity recognition, video understanding and deep learning.

The goal of my research is to build life long learning systems that learn with minimal or no human supervision. I utilize the structure inherently present in the data to learn visual representations that are transferable to several tasks. I also work on video understanding where we can exploit spatio-temporal relationships in the data to for machine learning tasks. Another goal is to create a system that can learn from very long term sequences (eg movies, textbooks). My past work also involved pose estimation from depth, video classification, emotion recognition, text segmentation and activity recognition. The reason I love this area, is because a lot of intuition of ML/DL algorithms comes from human cognitive system and psychology.
