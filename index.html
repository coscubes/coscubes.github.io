About

I am Kaustubh, and I am a computer vision enthusiast working at DreamVu as a computer Vision engineer. My research interest lie in computer vision, machine learning, deep learning and robotics. More specifically I am interested in action recognition, video classification, segmentation and other problems in visual recognition. In my free time I watch football, play thr guitar and read interesting stuff.

Research Interests

My long term research goal is to develop video understanding architectures using deep learning and computer vision for applications like action classification, action detection/localization, video object tracking and video segmentation. I am also interested in unsupervised methods and trying to develop weakly supervised methods for deep learning and eliminating the need for having large curated datasets (Never ending learning and online learning too). Other interests are:
Action recognition:- Action recognition, detection, classification, event recognition, behavior analysis, activity monitoring, muti-sensor fusion
Scene understanding:- Temporal reasoning (my favourite), Segmentation, Pose estimation, object detection, video classification. Deep learning:- Representation learning, Optimization, unsupervised and weakly supervised learning.
Other topics that interest me:- Depth Estimation, information retrieval, GANs, seq2seq.
