Kaustubh Sakhalkar

About

I am a computer vision engineer at DreamVu Inc. from June'18. I graduated from VIT University in 2018 with a Bachelors degree in computer science and bioinformatics.

Research Interests

I have profound interest in the areas of computer vision, deep learning statistical machine learning and language. Particularly I focus in the areas of action/activity recognition, pose estimation, video understanding, unsupervised visual discovery, active learning, vision and language. My long term research goal is to find the bridge between vision and language trying to learn relations that describe and understand the scene. I am also interested in zero shot and unsupervised learning in computer vision since high quality labelled data is expensive to obtain and maintain.
